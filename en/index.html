Questions

Is Tokyo Quest Log free?

Yes. Playing is free, and the gacha is free too — tickets are earned by exploring, never sold.

Do I need to download an app?

No. It's a web app: open tokyoquestlog.com and play. If you like it, add it to your home screen — it behaves like an app.

I'm only in Tokyo for a few days. Where do I start?

Pick a neighbourhood — Asakusa, Ueno or Akihabara are packed with quests — and clear it corner by corner. Landmark quests and limited-time exhibitions are marked clearly on the map.

What kinds of places are quests?

Art exhibitions, museums, anime and game spots, fashion and film events, workshops, shrines, towers and strange little museums — 400+ across greater Tokyo, from Hachioji to Yokohama.
